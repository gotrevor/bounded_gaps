  theorem `#print axioms = [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`, no sorry, no native_decide).
- **The engine** is `prod_sum_active_expand` (and its weighted twin): a `Fintype`-indexed
  generalization of the single-coordinate `divisor_pair_expand`. Recipe (now also in KB
  `[[lean-multidim-divisor-lattice-swap]]`): `Finset.sum_filter` → `Finset.prod_univ_sum` →
  `Fintype.prod_ite_zero` → `Finset.sum_comm` → `sum_const`/`sum_mul`. This is what turns
  `∑_{n∈block} ∏ᵢ(λλ)` into `∑_P (∏ μF μF)·#{lattice count}`.
- **KEY correction to the burndown** (was: sub-step (b) is "elementary, build it"): **mathlib
  HAS the pieces** — `Nat.Ioc_filter_modEq_card` (exact interval AP count) and the multi-modulus
  CRT (`Nat.chineseRemainderOfList`, `modEq_list_map_prod_iff`, `modEq_and_modEq_iff_modEq_mul`).
  So (b)'s diagonal count is mathlib-backed, not a build-from-scratch. Saved in KB
  `[[mathlib-has-ap-count-and-multimod-crt]]`. The genuinely-hard remnant of (b) is the
  **off-diagonal** (`gcd(qᵢ,qⱼ)>1`) error term — the bridge into (c).
- **Why both weights:** `selberg_nu k J c Fs H R = (∑ⱼ cⱼ ∏ᵢ λ_{Fⱼᵢ})²`. The separable case
  (J=1, `selberg_nu_separable`) is `(∏λ)²`; the general case opens via `sum_mul_sum` +
  `prod_mul_distrib` into a finite `(j,j')` sum of two-family products `∏ᵢ(λ_{Fⱼᵢ}·λ_{Fⱼ'ᵢ})`,
  each handled by the two-family lemma. s2 is identical with a `primeTheta(m+h_{i₀})` weight
  (the weighted workhorse).
- **Decisions/dead-ends:** (1) Put the work in a NEW file (not Sieve.lean) to avoid recompiling

  headlines — `exact` fails on a hidden `Finset.filter` Decidable-instance mismatch that prints
  identically (KB `[[lean-convert-absorbs-filter-decidable-mismatch]]`). (3) `Finset.sum_product'`
  won't match `f x.1 x.2` (projections share a bound var, not a Miller pattern) — use unprimed
  `Finset.sum_product`.
