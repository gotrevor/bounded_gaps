## 🧠 Context to carry forward
- **The key subtlety that the prior handoff worried about ("no clean inner-sum-constant analog")
  turned out tractable.** Individual per-`i` numerator terms genuinely are NOT orbit-invariant (the
  `(pᵢ+1)(qᵢ+1)` denominators and `removeNth i` couple to `i`). But the **sum over `i` IS
  invariant**: reindexing the inner `q`-sum by `q ↦ q ∘ σ` (`monoOrbit_image_comp` +
  `dirichletIntegralWithSlack_removeNth_perm_add`) turns the `i`-term at rep `α∘σ` into the
  `(σ i)`-term at rep `α`; then reindexing the outer `i`-sum by `σ` (`Fintype.sum_equiv`)
  re-collects them. Two reindexings, mirrors `polynomialMaynardNumerator_permWeight`'s shape.
- **Stated for `n+1`, not general `k`** — `Fin.removeNth i` needs `Fin (succ _)`, so a general-`k`
  statement doesn't even typecheck (unlike the denominator, which uses `monomialIntegral`, total in
  `k`). The `k=0` numerator is `0` by def anyway, so no content is lost.
- **The next step (#3) is the real wall — and the design doc says "bank, don't build" by default.**
  The cross-orbit overlap-matching closed form (`Mr[λ][μ](k) = (1/aut)·∑_M ff(k,T)·W(M)/(k+|λ|+|μ|)!`)
  is a **parametric-in-`k` symmetric-function integration identity**: mathlib-grade combinatorics,
  genuinely multi-session, degree-independent in difficulty. Per `tools/mk/SYMMETRIC_REDUCTION.md`,
  it buys ONLY the **EH-conditional** `mk_*` ladder (M_54>4, M_5511>6, …), NOT the unconditional
  flagship H₁≤246 (that needs the independent `Mk_eps` polynomial bridge, separately tracked in
  `EpsScaling.lean`/`EpsBridge.lean`). And even the first rung (M_54>4) needs a high-degree witness
  (≥9, likely teens) because true M_54 is only marginally >4. **So whether to build #3 at all is a
  real decision for Trevor, not an automatic next step.**
