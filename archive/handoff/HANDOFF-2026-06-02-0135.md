## 🔧 Lean gotchas hit this session
- **`Finset.card_bij'` goal order is ambiguous** — don't use positional `·` bullets; use `case hi =>`,
  `case hj =>`, `case left_inv =>`, `case right_inv =>`. (Lost two iterations to mis-ordered bullets.)
- **`set X := …` introduces a beta-redex** (`(fun v b => …) ⟨c,h⟩ ⟨b,h⟩`) that blocks `rw` whose pattern
  is the reduced form. Fix: prove by a direct term (defeq accepts it) or `change`/`show` to the reduced
  goal first. The lint also prefers `change` over `show` when the goal genuinely changes (up to defeq).
- **Multiset count ↔ Finset fiber**: `Multiset.count b (map f s) = card (s.filter (b = f ·))`
  (`Multiset.count_map`), then `← Finset.filter_val, ← Finset.card_def` bridges `univ.val.filter` to
  `(univ.filter ·).card`.
