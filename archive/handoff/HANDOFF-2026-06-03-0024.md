## 🧠 Context to carry forward (READ THIS — it overturns the prior handoffs)
The handoffs dated through 2026-06-02 claimed the endgame was "just data + `native_decide`,
host-better, box may OOM." **That is wrong, and the reason matters.** The committed `gram*Entry`
defs enumerate `MarginCorrectTables = univ.filter` over `CTable = img(α)→img(β)→Fin(k+1)`, whose
`univ` has cardinality **`(k+1)^(cells)`**. Every real witness needs orbits with ≥3 distinct exponent
values (img ≥ 3 ⇒ ≥9 cells), so this is ~`10²²`. **Verified empirically**: `#eval` of a single img-3
`MarginCorrectTables.card` does NOT finish even at **k=6** (40M-table filter with a costly per-table
predicate). So no amount of "host vs box" helps — the *architecture* had to change.

Mathematical lay of the land (all via `tools/mk/`, exact LDL):
- Infinite-dim `M_k` crosses 4 only near `k≈50`, so the named flagships `k=50/54` are razor-thin
  (`M_54≈4.0024`) ⇒ need degree ~20+ (≈600 orbits). Heavier; separate push.
- Larger k clears 4 at low degree: `D=7` (45 orbits), `M_200(7)≈4.003`, `M_300(7)≈4.011`. The exact
  LDL witness at k=300 has Rayleigh quotient `4.00694 > 4` — a real rational certificate.
- **No small-witness shortcut**: the sup builds *collectively* (greedy 31/45 orbits → only 3.78;
  full 45 → 4.011). So a small subset with one cheap-to-enumerate img-4 orbit does NOT clear 4 —
  the discharge needs ~all 45 orbits (2025 Gram entries). (`tools/mk/subset.py`.)

Two reformulations landed this lap (both verified `= gramDenEntry`/`gramNumEntry`):
- **Bounded** (`CTableBdd`, `gram*EntryBdd`): entries `Fin(min(rowM,colM)+1)` — img-3 enum
  `(k+1)⁹ → (k+1)·2⁸`. Made img-3 entries #eval-able at k=50 (were not).
- **Fréchet-windowed** (`CTableW`, `entryW=cellLo+raw`, `gram*EntryW`): `marginCorrect_frechet`
  (`T v b ≥ rowM+colM−k`) caps the dominant 0-cell's free range at `k−max(margins)=#nonzero≤2deg`,
  so the table count is **fully k-independent**. This is the witness shape (`Mk_gt_of_symWeight_witness_W`).

**The remaining bottleneck is now precisely "factor 1"**: with table *count* fixed, the dominant
cost is that `entryW`/`cellLo` and the margin filter recompute `(univ.filter (a i = v)).card` over
`Fin k` **per cell, per table** (~O(k) each, in the hot loop). At k=300 a full-witness `native_decide`
is ~10¹⁰ ops (~tens of min–hrs); hoisting those cards to O(1) literal lookups (~75×) ⇒ ~10⁸ ⇒
seconds–minutes. The bignums (factor 3) are secondary — they only hit the *accepted* tables, and
mathlib's `Nat.multinomial_cons` already gives a Pascal recurrence. So **A.1 (filter-card hoist) is
the single gating step for a kernel-checked `M_k > 4`.**
