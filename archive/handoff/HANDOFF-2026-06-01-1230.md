## ⚠️ Gotchas (this session)
- **`Fin.removeNth i` forces `Fin (succ _)`** — numerator-side orbit lemmas must be stated at `n+1`,
  not general `k` (the denominator side is fine at general `k`). The `k=0` numerator is `0` by def.
- **`rw` is syntactic; orbit terms are only defeq.** `(a∘σ)+(b∘σ)` vs `(a+b)∘σ`, `(p+q) i` vs
  `p i + q i` — bind the perm/sum lemma through a `have : <my exact syntactic form> := lemma` then
  `rw [that have]`, or close defeq gaps with `simp only [Pi.add_apply]`.
- **`unfold MultiIndex.degree` expands ALL `.degree`s** (incl. `α.degree`, `β.degree`) → use
  `show (∑ i, … i) = α.degree + β.degree` to keep the RHS folded, then rewrite the bare sums.
- Nat associativity bites: `k+(α.deg+β.deg)` (from `(p+q).degree`) vs `(k+α.deg)+β.deg` (the natural
  left-assoc statement) — close with `← add_assoc`.
