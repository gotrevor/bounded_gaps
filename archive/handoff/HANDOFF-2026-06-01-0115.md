- **The verdict (do not re-litigate):** *None* of the three papers' **prime-gap conclusions** is
  strictly formalizable today — all bottom out on a primes-in-APs-on-average input that mathlib
  lacks. Verified by grepping the pinned mathlib clone (`.lake/packages/mathlib`): **no
  Bombieri–Vinogradov, no large sieve, no Siegel–Walfisz**. Maynard-600 needs BV; Zhang-70M needs
  MPZ (Deligne-level) — so **Zhang is the *least* feasible, not more.** Don't pitch Zhang.
- **What IS feasible — the chosen target:** Maynard's **variational core `M_k → ∞`** — pure real
  analysis on the simplex, *no primes, no BV*. Crucially the expensive (measure-theory) half is
  **already done & axiom-clean in this repo**: `SievePolynomial.Mk_ge_polynomialMkF` gives
  `Mk k ≥ (polynomialMkF P : ℝ)` for any polynomial `P`, and the ratio is explicit factorials
  (`monomialIntegral`, `dirichletIntegralWithSlack`, both proven). So `M_k → ∞` reduces to a
  **purely algebraic** statement: exhibit a polynomial family whose factorial-ratio → ∞.
- **Trevor's constraint:** he chose this (Option B in the scope doc) and said **avoid
  `native_decide`** — so the `Mk5Witness`-style fixed-`k` compute route (Option A) is off the
  table; we want the genuine asymptotic.
- **The math spine** (what Aristotle / a future session must crack): use a product family
  `F = ∏ g(tᵢ)` (a function of `∑tᵢ` alone provably can't beat 4 — the GPY wall — so it MUST be
  multivariate); evaluate the separable simplex integral via the **Dirichlet–Gamma** connection
  (iid Exp(1)) or direct **Stirling**; optimize `g ≈ 1/(1+A t)`, `A≈log k`. A *crude* `≳ log k`
  (no sharp constant) suffices for `Tendsto … atTop`. mathlib HAS the tools:
  `Real.tendsto_stirlingSeq_sqrt_pi`, the Gamma function, and `Probability.Distributions.Gamma`.
- **Why Aristotle, why now:** Trevor said "pitch it and see what it can see." It's a legit
  leverage-over-an-architected-step use. Expect one of: full proof (unlikely first try), a
  partial with the construction + the hard asymptotic open, or a missing-lemma report — all useful
  signal on whether Option B is worth a real multi-month push.
